# Task 4: Brief Conclusions on Advertising Campaign Performance

## Analysis and Findings

**Campaigns to Scale:**

**Google Ads: UAC – Value | Android | APAC | Video**

**Why?** This campaign demonstrates the highest ROAS_D7 across both platforms at **1166.04%**. It also has a very low CPI (**$1.89**) and relatively low spend (**$3,087**). This indicates high efficiency and significant potential for budget increases without a substantial loss in profitability.

**Facebook Ads: UA – Retargeting – Value | iOS | LATAM | UGC**

**Why?** The campaign shows an outstanding ROAS_D7 of **1121.52%** with a moderate CPI (**$2.25**). Its costs are minimal (**$1,157**), suggesting that the budget can be confidently increased. Retargeting campaigns with such metrics are a primary driver of growth.

**Google Ads: UAC – Value | Android | EU | Playables**

**Why?** Despite already significant spending (**$13,970**), the campaign maintains an excellent ROAS_D7 of **1018.53%** and has a high number of Installs (**6,167**). This proves that it is not only effective but also highly scalable, retaining strong profitability as investment grows.

---

**Campaigns to Pause/Relaunch:**

**Google Ads: Search – Brand – Exact | iOS | US | UGC**

**Why?** This campaign has the lowest ROAS_D7 among those analyzed (**307.30%**) and the highest CPI (**$4.67**). For a brand campaign, which should attract the most loyal users, such metrics are unacceptable. It should be stopped immediately to analyze the reasons for its low performance (creatives, targeting).

**Facebook Ads: UA – Prospecting – Broad | IOS | US | USG**

**Why?** This is one of the most expensive campaigns on Facebook (**$17,922**), but its ROAS_D7 is extremely low at **443.07%**, with a CPI of **$3.76**. This confirms its inefficiency. The campaign should be paused to revise the audience and creatives.

**Facebook Ads: UA – Prospecting – Broad | Android | LATAM | Static**

**Why?** A similar situation: high costs (**$12,033**) yield a ROAS_D7 of only **431.82%**, which is significantly below average. The campaign is clearly not providing a return on investment and is spending a budget that could be reallocated to more successful campaigns. A pause for a complete overhaul is recommended.
